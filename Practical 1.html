Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max

The iPhone 18 Pro Max features a 6.9-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display with 120Hz ProMotion, a Ceramic Shield front and back, and an aluminum unibody available in Black, Silver, Glacier, and Burgundy. Powered by the A20 Pro chip with a vapor chamber cooling system and C2 modem, it delivers strong sustained performance, on-device AI capabilities, and up to 43 hours of video playback. Its triple-camera setup includes a 48MP Fusion main camera with a variable aperture, a 48MP Ultra Wide lens, and a 48MP Telephoto lens, starting at ₹1,79,900 in India

Display:
6.9-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display, 2868 × 1320-pixel resolution at 460 ppi, ProMotion 120Hz adaptive refresh rate, Dynamic Island, Always-On display, and up to 3000 nits peak outdoor brightness, protected by Ceramic Shield 2.
Processor & Performance:
Apple A20 Pro chip (2nm hexa-core CPU, 7-core GPU, dual 16-core Neural Engine), Apple C2 modem, Apple N1 wireless chip, 2nd-gen Ultra Wideband, and 12GB RAM.
Battery & Charging:
Battery & Charging: ~5,567 mAh battery supporting up to 43 hours of video playback, fast charging up to 60W (approx. 50% in 15 minutes), and MagSafe/Qi2 wireless charging up to 25W.
